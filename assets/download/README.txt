SkyrimYacht 0.1.3 (test build)
==============================

A game of Yacht (Yahtzee) you play against Skyrim's NPCs, with a cup you shake and dice that tumble.

Requirements: SKSE64 and Address Library for SKSE Plugins, for your game version. One DLL works on 1.5.97 and
every 1.6 release (Steam and GOG).

How to play: find someone sitting near a table in an inn and ask "Care for a Yacht game?". Bet 25 to 100 septims.
The game pauses and the world blurs behind the table.
  - Hold the left mouse button and move to shake the cup; let go to throw.
  - Click a die to keep it on your rack; drag dice back into the cup to roll them again.
  - Click a box on your card to score.  Z: Fus Ro Dah (shouts the dice back into the cup).  Tab or Esc: forfeit.

Settings: Data\SKSE\Plugins\SkyrimYacht.ini. If the table shows from underneath, set FlipTilt = 1.
Log: Documents\My Games\Skyrim Special Edition\SKSE\SkyrimYacht.log
